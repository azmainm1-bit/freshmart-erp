## Media provenance

The screenshots were supplied in the source archive and show the application with demo fixtures. They predate this package's small login, chart-label and accessibility refinements. They are not generated UI mockups and have not been presented as fresh end-to-end verification.

The MP4 uses those screenshots with captions and transitions. It is a screenshot-based product tour, not a live transaction recording. No real customer information or production credentials were added to the media. Source screenshots are retained at their original dimensions.

## Suggested live demonstration

After completing local setup, sign in as `manager1` with `manager123`:

1. Open **Store overview** and explain sales, inventory alerts and outstanding accounts.
2. Review **Products**, then show stock by location and batch.
3. Open **Point of sale**, open a till if needed, add an item and review the total before posting.
4. Complete a small demo cash sale and inspect its receipt in **Sales history**.
5. Revisit stock, show the related movement and explain the audit trail.
6. Open **Reports**, filter a period and export a CSV.
7. Close the till and review expected versus counted cash.

Perform this only against the local seeded demo. A recorded demonstration of these steps is the next media improvement once the full application runtime is available.
